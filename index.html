How to progress

    On rest days

    • Walk 30 minutes or hit about 8,000 steps. Log it as Cardio for XP.
    • Mobility: 10 minutes of stretching counts toward the mobility quest.
    • Train 3 days a week with a rest day between, like Mon, Wed, Fri or Tue, Thu, Sat.

    This week's meals

    Cook Sunday, reheat all week

    Sunday prep

    About 90 minutes

    Fridge portions are good through Wednesday. Freeze the Thursday–Saturday portions on Sunday and move each one to the fridge the night before.

    Cutting the puffiness

    • Sodium is the big one. Chips, deli meat, cheese, canned soup and fast food hold water. The prep recipes use salt-free seasoning and no-salt-added cans.
    • Alcohol and short sleep both show up in your face the next morning.
    • Move during the workday. Sitting for hours lets fluid pool, so take a short walk every hour or two.
    • Body fat is the rest. Face fat goes as overall body fat drops. Fat doesn't turn into muscle; you lose one and build the other at the same time.
    • See a doctor if swelling comes on suddenly, is only in one leg, or comes with shortness of breath or chest pain.
